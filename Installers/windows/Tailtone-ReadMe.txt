TAILTONE - kick punch + tail instrument by Jei Audio (Jeipster)
=================================================================

Tailtone is a BETA. Please report problems, crashes and sounds that come out
wrong to: Jeiaudio.project@gmail.com

What was installed
------------------
- VST3 plugin:  C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\Tailtone.vst3
- Standalone app (if selected):  C:\Program Files\Jei Audio\Tailtone\Tailtone.exe
Requirements: Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and a VST3 host (FL Studio, Ableton
Live, Reaper, Bitwig, Cubase, Studio One, ...).

First start
-----------
1. Let your DAW rescan its plugins. FL Studio: Options > Manage plugins >
   Find installed plugins. Tailtone is listed as an instrument (Jei Audio).
2. Load a punch and a tail: drag WAV or MP3 files onto the Punch and Tail
   panels, or use their Load buttons.
3. Check TAIL ROOT (the note of the tail; Tailtone detects it when the tail
   loads), then play MIDI notes. The Final Kick waveform shows what you hear; EXPORT WAV or
   DRAG takes the finished kick out.
Tailtone prepares the pitched notes in the background after a load. The bar
at the bottom right shows how many keys are ready.

Updates
-------
Run the new installer. It replaces the installed version, and projects saved
with earlier versions open unchanged.

Windows SmartScreen
-------------------
The installer is not code-signed yet, so Windows may show "Windows protected
your PC". Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

Uninstall
---------
Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps > Tailtone > Uninstall. Your settings
(theme, custom defaults) stay in %APPDATA%\JeiAudio\Tailtone.settings.
