KICKTONE - kick punch + tail instrument by Jei Audio (Jeipster)
=================================================================

Kicktone is a BETA. Please report problems, crashes and sounds that come out
wrong to: Jeiaudio.project@gmail.com

What was installed
------------------
- VST3 plugin:  C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\Kicktone.vst3
- Standalone app (if selected):  C:\Program Files\Jei Audio\Kicktone\Kicktone.exe
Requirements: Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and a VST3 host (FL Studio, Ableton
Live, Reaper, Bitwig, Cubase, Studio One, ...).

First start
-----------
1. Let your DAW rescan its plugins. FL Studio: Options > Manage plugins >
   Find installed plugins. Kicktone is listed as an instrument (Jei Audio).
2. Load a punch and a tail: drag WAV or MP3 files onto the Punch and Tail
   panels, or use their Load buttons.
3. Check TAIL ROOT (the note of the tail; Kicktone detects it when the tail
   loads), then play MIDI notes. The Final Kick waveform shows what you hear; EXPORT WAV or
   DRAG takes the finished kick out.
Kicktone prepares the pitched notes in the background after a load. The bar
at the bottom right shows how many keys are ready.

Updates
-------
Run the new installer. It replaces the installed version, and projects saved
with earlier versions open unchanged.

Kicktone was called Tailtone before version 0.5.29. The installer removes the
old Tailtone.vst3 so your DAW lists the plugin only once. In FL Studio, rescan
your plugins once to see the new name.

Windows SmartScreen
-------------------
The installer is not code-signed yet, so Windows may show "Windows protected
your PC". Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

Uninstall
---------
Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps > Kicktone > Uninstall. Your settings
(theme, custom defaults) stay in %APPDATA%\JeiAudio\Kicktone.settings.
